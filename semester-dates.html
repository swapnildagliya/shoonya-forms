Catch-up week

The week after each semester ends — reserved for giving back a class that was cancelled through illness or injury.

Free trial week

In the first week of a semester, students may attend a class free of charge before they register.

Holiday weeks

No classes run during Herfst-, Kerst- or Paasvakantie. They are already excluded from the session counts.
